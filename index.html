Project Writeups

  • Cyclone Intensity Model
    Used the SHIPS cyclone dataset and a Bayesian hierarchical model to predict the maximum sustained wind speed 36 hours after a cyclone is first detected. The primary advantage of a Bayesian model for this task was its ability to represent uncertainty, which would allow the NHC or other agencies to make good decisions about what recommendations to make. Toy decision analysis is included in Section 4.3.
  • Modeled the Great Salt Lake's volume with a salinity-aware differential equation that closely tracked 24 years of history when forced with observed inflow, then used Monte Carlo inflow simulations to estimate recovery odds under different water-diversion policies.
